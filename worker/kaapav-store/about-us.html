⚡ The Founder's Journey: Built with Vision & Zero Code

Behind KAAPAV is an authentic origin story. This entire brand, its intelligent end-to-end WhatsApp commerce automations, and this custom e-commerce web platform were built from the ground up by a passionate non-coder founder in Bengaluru.

Driven by a singular mission to cut out traditional retail middlemen and overcome technical barriers without a software background, we engineered seamless WhatsApp ordering, real-time catalogue synchronisation, and live order tracking.

By pairing technology with artisanal craftsmanship, we keep contemporary, high-finish jewellery directly reachable, affordable, and accessible to women across all 19,000+ pin codes in India.
